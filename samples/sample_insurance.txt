SURAKSHA HEALTH PLUS: POLICY TERMS (EXCERPT)

This excerpt summarises the key terms of the Suraksha Health Plus individual
health insurance policy ("Policy") issued by Suraksha General Insurance
Limited ("Insurer") to the policyholder ("You").

1. WHAT IS COVERED. The Insurer will pay the reasonable and customary cost of
in-patient hospitalisation up to the Sum Insured stated in the Schedule,
provided the hospitalisation lasts at least 24 consecutive hours and is
medically necessary. Day-care procedures listed in Annexure A are covered
without the 24 hour requirement.

2. INITIAL WAITING PERIOD. No claim is payable for any illness that begins
within the first thirty (30) days after the Policy start date. This waiting
period does not apply to hospitalisation caused by an accident.

3. SPECIFIC WAITING PERIOD. Claims for cataract, hernia, and joint replacement
surgery are not payable until twenty-four (24) months of continuous coverage
have been completed, unless the surgery is needed because of an accident.

4. PRE-EXISTING DISEASES. Any condition You had or were diagnosed with before
the Policy start date is covered only after thirty-six (36) months of
continuous coverage. These conditions must have been declared in the proposal
form. Non-disclosure may lead to rejection of the claim.

5. ROOM RENT LIMIT. The Insurer will pay room rent up to one percent (1%) of
the Sum Insured per day. If You choose a room with higher rent, all other
hospital charges will be reduced in the same proportion.

6. CO-PAYMENT. If the insured person is aged sixty (60) years or more at the
time of the claim, You must bear ten percent (10%) of every admissible claim
amount. No co-payment applies below that age.

7. HOW TO CLAIM. For a planned hospitalisation, You must inform the Insurer at
least three (3) days before admission. For an emergency hospitalisation, You
must inform the Insurer within forty-eight (48) hours of admission. Cashless
treatment is available only at network hospitals; at other hospitals You must
pay first and submit the bills for reimbursement within thirty (30) days of
discharge.

8. EXCLUSIONS. The Policy does not cover cosmetic or plastic surgery unless
needed after an accident or to treat cancer, dental treatment unless caused by
an accident, treatment taken outside India, or any hospitalisation mainly for
investigation or evaluation without active treatment.

9. RENEWAL AND NO-CLAIM BONUS. The Policy is renewable for life. A grace
period of thirty (30) days is allowed after the due date, and the Policy is
not in force during the grace period. For every claim-free year the Sum
Insured increases by five percent (5%), up to a maximum of fifty percent
(50%). The bonus is reduced if a claim is made.
