RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 25 October 2026
between Mr. R. Sharma ("Landlord") and Ms. A. Verma ("Tenant") for the
residential flat at 14, Lakeview Apartments, Bengaluru ("Premises").

1. TERM. This Agreement runs for eleven (11) months, starting on 1 November
2026 and ending on 30 September 2027, unless ended earlier under Clause 6.

2. RENT. The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 32,000 on or before the 5th
day of each month by bank transfer. If rent is unpaid after the 10th day of the
month, a late fee of Rs. 200 per day applies until the rent is paid. The rent
shall remain fixed for the Term and shall not be increased during the Term.

3. MAINTENANCE. The Tenant shall pay a fixed maintenance charge of Rs. 2,500
per month to the apartment association. Minor repairs costing less than
Rs. 1,000 are the Tenant's responsibility. All structural repairs and repairs
to plumbing and electrical wiring are the Landlord's responsibility.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of
Rs. 96,000 (three months' rent) on signing this Agreement. The Landlord shall
refund the Security Deposit, without interest, within thirty (30) days after
the Tenant vacates the Premises and returns all keys, subject to the
deductions permitted under Clause 5.

5. PERMITTED DEDUCTIONS. The Landlord may deduct from the Security Deposit
only the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear and any unpaid
rent or utility charges. The Landlord must provide an itemised statement of
every deduction, with supporting bills, when the balance is refunded.

6. NOTICE AND TERMINATION. Either party may end this Agreement by giving the
other party sixty (60) days' written notice. If the Tenant leaves before the
end of the Term without giving the required notice, the Tenant shall pay rent
in lieu of notice for the unexpired part of the notice period.

7. ACCESS BY LANDLORD. The Landlord may enter the Premises only after giving
the Tenant at least 24 hours' notice, except in an emergency such as fire or
a burst water pipe.

8. USE AND SUBLETTING. The Premises shall be used only as a private residence
for the Tenant and her immediate family. The Tenant shall not sublet the
Premises or any part of it, and shall not keep pets without the Landlord's
written consent.

9. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the
courts at Bengaluru shall have jurisdiction over any dispute.
